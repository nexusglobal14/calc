About the Advanced Age & Date Calculator

This age calculator works out your exact age from your date of birth. You can measure your age as of today or as of any other date. Besides years, months and days, it shows total months, weeks, days, hours, minutes and seconds lived, how long until your next birthday, the weekday you were born on and your Western zodiac sign.

How to use it

  1. Select your date of birth.
  2. Leave the second date as today, or choose a different date to calculate age at that moment.
  3. Click Calculate Exact Age and read your results.
  4. Use Copy Summary to copy the results.

The formula

The calculator subtracts the date of birth from the target date in calendar order. It counts whole years first, then remaining whole months, then days, borrowing days from the previous month when needed. Total days are the difference in calendar days between the two dates, and total weeks, hours, minutes and seconds follow from that.

Worked example

Someone born on March 15, 2000 would be exactly 26 years old on March 15, 2026, and 26 years, 8 months and 0 days on November 15, 2026.

Things to keep in mind

Totals for hours, minutes and seconds count full days from midnight to midnight and do not account for birth time or time zone. Leap years are handled using the Gregorian calendar rules. Zodiac and generation labels are shown for general interest only.

Frequently asked questions

How is exact age calculated?

By counting complete years, then complete months, then the remaining days between the two dates.

Can I calculate age at a past or future date?

Yes. Change the second date to any date you like.

How are leap years handled?

The calculator uses the Gregorian calendar, so February 29 is counted in leap years.

Does it know my birth time?

No. It works with dates only, so hour and minute totals are approximate.
