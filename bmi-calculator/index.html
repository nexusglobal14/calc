About the BMI Body Mass Index Calculator

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a simple number that relates weight to height. It is widely used as a screening tool for adults. Enter your weight in kilograms and your height in centimeters to see your BMI and the matching category.

How to use it

  1. Enter your weight in kilograms.
  2. Enter your height in centimeters.
  3. Read your BMI and its category.

The formula

BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)2. Height in centimeters is divided by 100 to convert it to meters. The adult categories used here are: under 18.5 underweight; 18.5 to 24.9 normal weight; 25 to 29.9 overweight; 30 and above obese.

Worked example

A person who weighs 70 kg and is 170 cm (1.70 m) tall has a BMI of 70 ÷ 2.89 = 24.2, which falls in the normal weight range.

Things to keep in mind

BMI is a screening measure, not a diagnosis. It does not distinguish muscle from fat, does not consider where fat is carried and may not suit athletes, older adults, pregnant women or children. Different populations can also have different recommended ranges. These categories apply to adults only.

Frequently asked questions

What is a healthy BMI range?

For most adults, 18.5 to 24.9 is classed as normal weight by the WHO.

Is BMI accurate for athletes?

Often not. Muscle weighs more than fat, so muscular people can have a high BMI without excess body fat.

Can I use this for children?

No. Children and teenagers use age- and sex-specific percentile charts.

What should I do if my BMI is outside the normal range?

BMI alone does not say anything definitive about your health. Speak to a healthcare professional, who can consider other measurements and your overall health.
