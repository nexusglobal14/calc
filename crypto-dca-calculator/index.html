About the Crypto Profit Calculator

This calculator shows how much a crypto position would gain or lose if you bought at one price and sold at another. Enter your buy price, your sell price and the amount you invested. It shows how many coins you bought and the profit or loss in dollars.

How to use it

  1. Enter the price you bought at, per coin.
  2. Enter the price you expect to sell at, per coin.
  3. Enter the amount you invested in dollars.
  4. Read the number of coins and the total profit or loss.

The formula

Coins = investment ÷ buy price. Sale value = coins × sell price. Profit or loss = sale value − investment.

Worked example

You invest $1,000 at a buy price of $50,000 per coin and get 0.02 coins. Selling at $65,000 gives $1,300, a profit of $300 or 30%.

Things to keep in mind

Dollar-cost averaging (DCA) means buying a fixed amount at regular intervals. This tool models one purchase. To evaluate a DCA plan, divide your total dollars invested by the total coins you bought to get your average cost, and enter that average as the buy price. Trading fees, network fees and taxes are not included.

Frequently asked questions

What is dollar-cost averaging?

It is a strategy of investing a fixed amount on a regular schedule, regardless of price, so you buy more coins when prices are low and fewer when they are high.

How do I find my average buy price?

Divide the total money you spent by the total number of coins you own.

Are fees included?

No. Exchange and network fees will reduce your real profit.

Can I use this for stocks?

Yes. The same arithmetic applies to any asset bought at one price and sold at another.
