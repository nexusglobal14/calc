Free calculators for money, health and everyday math

Nexus Global Calculators is a collection of free tools that run entirely in your web browser. Nothing you type is sent to a server. The finance tools cover mortgage payments, SIP and compound interest growth, take-home pay, ROI and profit margin, and crypto trade profit. The health tools estimate BMI, daily calorie needs and pregnancy due dates, and the utility tools handle percentages and exact age.

Every calculator page explains the formula it uses, shows a worked example, lists its limitations and answers common questions, so you can understand the result and not only read a number.
