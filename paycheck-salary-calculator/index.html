About the Paycheck & Take-Home Salary Calculator

This calculator gives a quick estimate of your take-home pay. Enter your gross annual salary and one estimated overall tax and deduction rate, and it shows the total deducted and your net annual pay. It uses a single flat rate, so it is a rough planning tool rather than a payroll calculation.

How to use it

  1. Enter your gross annual salary before deductions.
  2. Enter an estimated combined tax and deduction rate as a percentage.
  3. Read the gross income, deductions and net annual pay.

The formula

Deductions = gross salary × (rate ÷ 100) and Net pay = gross salary − deductions. Divide the net figure by 12 for an approximate monthly amount, or by 26 or 52 for biweekly or weekly pay.

Worked example

With a gross salary of $60,000 and an estimated 20% rate, deductions are $12,000 and net annual pay is $48,000. That is about $4,000 per month.

Things to keep in mind

Real income tax is usually progressive, so higher portions of income are taxed at higher rates. Actual deductions also depend on your country, state, filing status, retirement contributions, health insurance and other items. Use your pay stub or your tax authority's tools for exact figures. This calculator does not look up any tax tables.

Frequently asked questions

What tax rate should I enter?

Look at a recent pay stub and divide total deductions by gross pay. Use that percentage as your estimated rate.

Does this calculate federal or state tax?

No. It applies one flat rate that you choose. It does not use tax brackets.

How do I get monthly take-home pay?

Divide the net annual pay by 12.

Is the result exact?

No. It is an estimate for planning purposes only.
