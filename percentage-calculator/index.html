About the Percentage Calculator

This calculator answers the most common percentage question: what is X percent of a number? Enter the percentage and the number, and the result appears instantly. It is useful for working out discounts, sales tax, tips, commissions and shares of a total.

How to use it

  1. Enter the percentage in the first field.
  2. Enter the number you want to take the percentage of.
  3. Read the result.

The formula

Result = (percentage ÷ 100) × number. For example, 15% of 250 is 0.15 × 250 = 37.5.

Worked example

A jacket costs $250 and is 15% off. The discount is 15% of 250 = $37.50, so you pay $212.50. For an 18% tip on a $60 bill, enter 18 and 60 to get $10.80.

Things to keep in mind

This tool calculates a percentage of a value. To find a percentage change, subtract the old value from the new one, divide by the old value and multiply by 100. For profit-related percentages, try the ROI and profit margin calculator.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate a discount?

Enter the discount percentage and the original price. The result is the amount saved. Subtract it from the price for the final cost.

How do I add a percentage to a number?

Calculate the percentage with this tool, then add the result to the original number. For example, 8% tax on $200 is $16, for a total of $216.

Can I enter decimals?

Yes. Both fields accept decimal values such as 7.5.

How do I find what percent one number is of another?

Divide the first number by the second and multiply by 100. For example, 30 of 120 is 30 ÷ 120 × 100 = 25%.
