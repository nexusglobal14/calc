About the ROI & Profit Margin Calculator

Return on investment (ROI) and profit margin are two of the most common ways to measure business performance, and they are easy to confuse. This calculator takes your total cost and total revenue and shows net profit, ROI and profit margin together.

How to use it

  1. Enter your total cost or investment.
  2. Enter your total revenue from that investment.
  3. Read the net profit, ROI percentage and profit margin.

The formula

Net profit = revenue − cost. ROI = (net profit ÷ cost) × 100. Profit margin = (net profit ÷ revenue) × 100. ROI compares profit to what you put in. Margin compares profit to what you took in.

Worked example

You spend $1,000 and earn $1,500 in revenue. Net profit is $500. ROI is 500 ÷ 1,000 = 50%. Profit margin is 500 ÷ 1,500 = 33.33%.

Things to keep in mind

Use the same time period and the same definition of cost for every comparison. Include all costs, such as materials, labor, advertising and fees, or the result will look better than reality. A negative result means a loss.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between ROI and profit margin?

ROI divides profit by cost. Margin divides profit by revenue. The same deal gives a higher ROI than margin whenever it is profitable.

What is a good ROI?

It depends on the industry, the risk and the time period. Compare against alternatives, not a single universal number.

Can ROI be negative?

Yes. If revenue is lower than cost, you made a loss and ROI is negative.

Does it include taxes?

No. It uses the cost and revenue figures you enter.
