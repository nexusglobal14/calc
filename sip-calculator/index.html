About the SIP Calculator

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) means investing a fixed amount at regular intervals, usually monthly, into a mutual fund or similar investment. This calculator estimates what a SIP could grow to if it earned a steady annual return. Enter your monthly amount, the expected annual return and the number of years.

How to use it

  1. Enter the amount you plan to invest every month.
  2. Enter the expected annual return as a percentage, for example 12.
  3. Enter the investment period in years.
  4. Read the invested amount, estimated returns and total value.

The formula

The future value is FV = P × [((1 + i)n − 1) ÷ i] × (1 + i), where P is the monthly investment, i is the monthly rate (annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100) and n is the number of months. The extra (1 + i) factor assumes each payment is made at the start of the month. Invested amount is P × n, and estimated returns are FV minus the invested amount.

Worked example

Investing $500 per month at 12% a year for 10 years means 120 payments and $60,000 invested. The estimated future value is about $116,170, of which about $56,170 is growth.

Things to keep in mind

Real investments do not earn a constant return. Market returns vary from year to year and can be negative, so treat the output as an illustration rather than a forecast. Fees, taxes and inflation are not included.

Frequently asked questions

Is the SIP return guaranteed?

No. The calculator assumes a constant rate. Actual market-linked returns vary and can be lower or negative.

What return rate should I enter?

Use a conservative figure you consider realistic for the asset type. Trying a few rates shows how sensitive the result is.

Why does starting early matter?

Returns compound, so money invested earlier has more time to grow. Try the same monthly amount over 10, 20 and 30 years to see the difference.

Can I use this for something other than mutual funds?

Yes. It works for any regular monthly investment with an assumed constant annual return.
